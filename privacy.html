Privacy Policy — Pin Potha

Effective date: 26 August 2026

Pin Potha is a personal good-deeds journal designed to work primarily on your device. This policy explains what information the app uses and how optional Google features work.

1. Information stored on your device

Good-deed entries and optional personal details such as name, phone number, address, and NIC are stored locally on your device. These personal-detail fields are optional.

2. Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is optional. If you choose to sign in, Pin Potha may receive basic account information needed to identify the signed-in Google account, such as your account identifier, display name, and email address.

3. Google Drive backup

If you enable backup, Pin Potha stores its backup data in Google Drive's app-specific hidden application-data area. The app requests access only to its own app data and does not request general access to your personal Google Drive files.

4. What is included in a backup

A backup may include your good-deed entries, their dates and times, record identifiers and update timestamps, and any optional personal details you chose to enter.

5. Data sharing

Pin Potha does not sell your personal data. It does not include advertising, social profiles, leaderboards, or location tracking. Data is only sent to Google services when you choose to use Google Sign-In or Google Drive backup.

6. PDF and printing

When you create a PDF or print your records, the resulting document is handled by Android's file and printing features. You control where the file is saved or sent.

7. Data deletion

You can delete good-deed records from within the app. You can also sign out of Google from the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local application data from the device, subject to Android's normal backup and restore behavior.

8. Security

Pin Potha is designed to request only the Google permissions needed for its backup feature. No system can guarantee absolute security, but the app avoids unnecessary permissions and does not include advertising or tracking SDKs.

9. Children

Pin Potha is a general-purpose personal journal and does not knowingly collect information for advertising or profiling of children.

10. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.

11. Contact and source code

For project information, issues, and source code, visit the Pin Potha GitHub repository.
