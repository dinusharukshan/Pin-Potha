Pin Potha

A free Android app for recording and preserving good deeds.

Pin Potha helps you keep a personal record of good deeds, search and filter entries, back them up to Google Drive, restore them on another device, and create printable A4 PDF records.

Features

  • Record, edit and delete good deeds
  • Offline-first local storage
  • Sinhala and English interface
  • Search and date filtering
  • Optional Google Sign-In
  • Google Drive app-data backup and restore
  • Automatic background backup
  • A4 PDF export and Android printing
  • Light and dark modes with accent colors
  • No ads and no subscriptions

Privacy

Your good-deed records are stored locally on your device. Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup are optional. Pin Potha uses Google Drive's app-specific hidden app-data area for backups.
